A NOTE ON MAKING · 01

Leave room for
the day to change.

We make places that notice the small things: the sun moving across a wall, a doorway that slows you down, the grain of a board worn smooth by daily life.

Each project begins with what is already there. We listen to a site, work with its materials, and shape rooms that feel generous without asking to be the center of attention.
